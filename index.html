How It Works

1

Type any academic or course-related question into the input box.

2

AI Classifies

Gemini decides: can AI handle this, or does it need a faculty member?

3

If AI can help, you get a detailed answer plus 3 learning resources.

4

Escalate if Needed

For grade disputes, personal issues, or admin queries — you're directed to a human.

⚠️ Known Limitations

This AI prototype is designed to assist, not replace, academic support. Be aware of the following:

🌀
Hallucination

The AI may occasionally generate incorrect or made-up information. Always verify important facts.

🧠
No Memory

Each question is answered independently. The AI has no memory of previous questions in the session.

🔒
No Access to Records

The AI cannot access student records, grades, timetables, or any institutional systems.

🔗
Resource URLs

Suggested resource links are AI-generated. While typically accurate, some links may not always be exact.
